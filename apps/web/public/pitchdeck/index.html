Privacy-First Architecture · Enterprise Wedge
Your content lives
in your Drive.
Not ours.

In an era of AI training scandals and data breaches, Iterra made a deliberate architectural choice: your data never touches our servers — enforced at the infrastructure level, not just policy.

🔒 The Enterprise Unlock
This privacy architecture is our wedge into B2B and Enterprise. Large brands and agencies cannot use tools that store their proprietary strategy and content on third-party servers. Iterra is the only AI strategy platform that passes enterprise security reviews out of the box — unlocking a customer segment worth 10× the SMB market.
Google Drive Storage
All drafts and brand profiles live in your Google Drive via OAuth. Revoke access anytime — your data stays yours.
Dual JWT Authentication
Supabase Auth paired with custom JWTs. Battle-tested passlib and python-jose. No proprietary auth risk — fully auditable.
Zero Raw LLM Retention
Every AI response is structured through Pydantic schemas. No raw strings stored, no prompts logged without consent.
The True Compounding Moat
Our privacy shield gets users in the door, but data gravity keeps them here. Iterra continuously learns from post performance. The longer they use it, the better it predicts their specific audience. Competitors offer a blank slate every day; we offer a compounding brain.
